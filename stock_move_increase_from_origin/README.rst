When the quantity of a move goes up, Odoo spreads it over the move lines
in order: each line takes the free stock of its own lot in the location
before the next line is checked, and the next lines are reduced if
needed. That free stock may belong to another chain of transfers. So a
chained move can take what another transfer brought and leave unused
what its own origin moves brought. In a manufacturing order this happens
with any quantity to produce, even below the planned one, because
setting it spreads the components again.

With this module, a chained move whose origin moves are done keeps its
move lines and takes the extra quantity from what its origin moves
brought, the same way Odoo reserves it. What they cannot cover is
reserved as usual, and only if there is no stock left Odoo adds a move
line without reservation. When the quantity goes down, the move only
releases stock, starting with the last reserved move lines, as Odoo does
when the quantity of a transfer is lowered.

Usage
=====

There is nothing to configure. For example, with two chained transfers
of 10 units:

1. The first transfer brings 8 units of lot A and 7 units of lot B to
   the next location.
2. The second transfer reserves 8 units of lot A and 2 units of lot B.
3. Another transfer left 2 free units of lot A in the same location.
4. Set the quantity of the second transfer to 12: the 2 extra units come
   from lot B, which its origin brought, instead of the free units of
   lot A.

In a manufacturing order whose components come from a done transfer,
setting a quantity to produce below the planned one only releases the
last reserved lots of the components.
